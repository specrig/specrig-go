**An event is said as what happened.** `The contract gets locked`, not `the event
arrives` under a `Given` that already holds the change. The step's binding makes the
neighbour answer accordingly and sends the message; the step says it in its reader's
words, not as a neighbour's answer. Where what changed does not matter, because the
service reads the state again, `the contract changes` is the whole of it and the state
stays in the `Given`. A bare delivery is written only where the delivery is the case: a
repeat, a broken message.

**What lands during an operation is a `Given`.** `That invoice gets paid while the move
is being priced` is a situation its reader recognises; which call opens the window is
the binding's.
